A [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca) entry for **Two Lines to Your Past Self**: two lines, a warning to my past self about one specific moment, and one sentence on why it mattered.

> Don't sell the old guitar to pay the rent in March — pawn the TV instead.
> That guitar is the one you play at your daughter's wedding, twenty years later.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the two lines byte-exact to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.

Run it locally with `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out node main.ts` — the output is exactly two lines, 153 characters, no trailing newline.
